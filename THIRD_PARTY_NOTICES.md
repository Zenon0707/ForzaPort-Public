ModelProbe/upstream parser dependencies, Syroot, Granny and XG libraries require their applicable upstream terms/permissions. The retained credits record provenance gaps; this notice index is not proof of blanket redistribution permission, nor does Rock's permission license Microsoft's XG library. Maintainer confirmation of separate permissions is not replaced by inventing an open-source licence for those binaries.

The development history was preserved in a separate private backup before this clean packaged release. The supplied runtime SOURCE.zip files remain colocated with their respective DLLs.
